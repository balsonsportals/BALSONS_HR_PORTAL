1. DOCUMENT CONTROL
2. EXECUTIVE REQUIREMENT
BALSONS requires a centralized HR and Payroll Manager to manage employee master data, project/site allocation, monthly attendance, overtime, leave, advances, deductions, payroll processing, salary slips, HR documents, employee exit/final settlement, dashboards, reports, approvals, audit and backup/recovery.
The system shall be delivered as a responsive HTML web application hosted from GitHub, with Google Apps Script providing all backend processing and Google Sheets serving as the live operational database. The system is online-first. It must provide controlled access to five user roles and preserve payroll calculation integrity, historical records and approval/locking controls.
This is a fresh Google Sheets + Apps Script + HTML web-app platform baseline. Earlier Excel/VBA/offline-only deployment constraints are superseded for this version. Excel files may be used for controlled import/export or migration where approved, but are not the live database.
3. PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE
3.1 Data flow
User browser → GitHub-hosted frontend → secure API communication layer → Apps Script backend → Google Sheets and restricted Drive.
Frontend must not connect directly to Google Sheets.
No payroll data, passwords, OAuth tokens, API keys or service credentials in public frontend code or Git.
Apps Script enforces authentication, role authorization, validation and payroll calculations server-side.
Google Sheets is not publicly shared to enable frontend access.
3.2 Cross-origin/API proof — mandatory Phase 0
GitHub Pages and Apps Script are different origins. Apps Script Web App redirect/response behaviour can make ordinary browser fetch/CORS integration unreliable. Do not assume a normal fetch implementation will work without testing.
Prove secure browser-to-Apps-Script communication from the actual GitHub Pages origin before full feature development.
Do not use `no-cors` for calls whose response must be read.
Do not use state-changing GET/JSONP calls as a substitute for authenticated POST operations.
Do not put session tokens or employee/payroll data in URL query strings.
If direct secure communication is not viable, submit a minimal gateway/proxy design for explicit approval; do not silently add another platform.
4. BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
Single authoritative employee and payroll data system.
Consistent monthly attendance and payroll processing.
Reduce manual calculations, duplicate data entry and spreadsheet errors.
Provide project-wise and employee-wise labour/payroll visibility.
Control salary advances, recoveries, deductions, leave and overtime.
Generate accurate individual and batch salary slips.
Manage HR documents and employee lifecycle records.
Enforce role-based access, review, approval, payroll locking and audit.
Provide management dashboards, statutory/management reports and reliable backup/recovery.
5. USER ROLES AND PERMISSIONS
All permissions must be enforced in Apps Script, not just by hiding UI controls.
Project Manager and Area Supervisor must be restricted to assigned projects/areas.
Employee role must be restricted to the employee's own records.
Sensitive salary, bank, identity and HR documents must be restricted by role.
Approval authority and edit rights must be separate where configured.
6. CORE MODULES
6.1 Employee Master and onboarding
Unique Employee ID; employee name; contact details; address and emergency contact.
Date of birth, date of joining, employment status and employment category as required.
Department, designation, trade/skill, grade and reporting supervisor.
Project/site allocation and allocation effective dates.
Identity and statutory identifiers only where required and access-controlled.
Bank/payment details with strict role restrictions; never display full account details unnecessarily.
Salary structure, wage basis, allowances and deduction configuration linked to effective dates.
Document checklist, joining status, active/inactive/left status and complete change history.
Create, edit, view, search, filter, deactivate and reactivate under controlled permissions.
6.2 Project, site and department master
Create and maintain project/site codes, names, locations and status.
Department, area, cost centre and designation masters.
Assign employees to projects/areas with start/end dates.
Support transfers and multiple allocations only if business rules explicitly permit.
Project Manager and Area Supervisor see only assigned scope.
6.3 Attendance management
Monthly attendance is a central payroll input. The system must support both manual attendance entry and controlled bulk/import workflows, subject to validation and approval.
Monthly attendance period and employee-wise attendance grid.
Required attendance codes/inputs include Present, WO (weekly off), PL (paid leave), OT (overtime) and Advance, consistent with the approved payroll rulebook.
Support daily entry and monthly summary views where configured.
Supervisor enters attendance for assigned workforce; Project Manager reviews/approves; HR validates and closes the period.
Prevent duplicate employee/date entries and invalid attendance combinations.
Track changes, remarks, submission, correction, approval and lock status.
Show missing attendance, abnormal totals, pending approvals and employees with no attendance.
Do not silently convert blank attendance into Present, WO, leave or absence.
6.4 Overtime management
Record overtime hours by employee/date/project/shift or approved payroll period.
Capture reason, work reference and submitting supervisor.
Apply configured OT eligibility, rate and calculation rules only after they are approved.
Project-level review and HR/payroll validation.
Prevent duplicate or unauthorized OT entries and retain approval history.
6.5 Leave management
Leave types, entitlement/accrual and carry-forward rules configurable after HR policy approval.
Leave application, date range, reason, supporting document reference where required.
Approval workflow by reporting authority/HR as configured.
Leave balance, approved, pending, rejected and cancelled transactions.
Integrate approved leave with attendance/payroll without double counting.
PL code must follow the approved payroll mapping; do not assume all leave categories are interchangeable.
6.6 Advance and deductions
Employee advance issue register: date, amount, project, reason, approved by and payment reference.
Recovery schedule, monthly deduction, recovered balance and outstanding amount.
Other authorized deductions with category, amount, period, reason and approval.
Prevent deduction exceeding permitted policy limits; exceptions require approval.
Reconcile opening balance + new advances − recovery = closing balance.
Maintain transaction history; do not overwrite prior recovery entries.
6.7 Salary structure and payroll configuration
Employee-wise salary/wage structure with effective-from/effective-to dates.
Earnings, allowances, overtime components and deductions as approved by BALSONS payroll policy.
Monthly paid days, payable days, attendance treatment, rounding and proration rules configurable.
Salary component mapping, calculation sequence and eligibility conditions documented.
Version all salary structures and retain historical rates for past payroll periods.
Restrict configuration changes to authorized roles and require approval where configured.
The exact payroll formulas, component definitions, attendance-to-payable-days mapping, OT rate, rounding and statutory treatment must be reconciled to the authoritative BALSONS payroll workbook and approved policy before implementation. This requirements document does not invent or replace those formulas.
6.8 Payroll processing engine
Select payroll month and applicable employee/project population.
Validate employee status, salary structure, attendance, leave, OT, advances and deductions.
Calculate gross earnings, each earning component, deductions, advance recovery and net payable using approved formulas.
Show employee-wise calculation breakdown and exception list.
Support draft calculation, recalculation before approval, review, approval and period lock.
Prevent duplicate payroll runs for the same employee and period unless authorized reversal/re-run is recorded.
Maintain payroll run ID, version, timestamps, preparer, reviewer, approver and lock status.
Compare current month with previous month and flag material variances for review.
Do not permit silent edits to approved/locked payroll; corrections use controlled adjustment/reversal workflow.
6.9 Salary slips / payslips
Generate individual salary slip for selected employee and month.
Generate all eligible salary slips in batch for a payroll period.
PDF output with BALSONS branding and approved salary slip layout.
Include employee identity/reference, designation/project where approved, pay period, attendance/payable days, earnings, deductions, advance recovery and net pay.
Amount in words if confirmed as part of approved payslip design.
Store generated file reference, payroll run ID, generation time and version in a payslip log.
Employee can access only own payslip; HR/Admin can access according to role.
Regenerate only under controlled rules; retain previous version history.
6.10 HR documents and employee file
Employee document register with document type, issue/expiry dates, verification status and file reference.
Support approved HR templates and generated documents such as appointment/engagement letters, employee ID/records, experience/service letters and other BALSONS-approved forms.
Template placeholders mapped to employee master data; preserve approved design and wording.
Generate PDF/print output and store restricted Drive references where enabled.
Document access restricted by role and employee ownership.
Document list and expiry alerts where applicable.
6.11 Exit and final settlement
Employee exit request, last working date, exit reason and approval.
Clearance checklist for company property/documents/advances as applicable.
Final attendance, leave adjustment, outstanding advance and authorized deductions.
Final settlement calculation using approved policy and effective salary rules.
Review/approval and settlement status tracking.
Generate final settlement statement and preserve audit history.
6.12 Dashboard and management reports
Headcount: active, inactive, joined and exited employees.
Project/site-wise workforce and allocation.
Monthly attendance summary, missing attendance and pending approvals.
Overtime hours/cost by employee, project and period.
Payroll gross, deductions, net and employee count by month/project.
Advance issued, recovered and outstanding balances.
Leave balances and leave transactions.
Payroll processing status: draft, pending review, approved, locked and slips generated.
HR document completeness/expiry and employee lifecycle reports.
Export permitted reports to Excel/CSV/PDF.
7. APPROVAL, LOCKING AND CONTROL WORKFLOW
The final approval matrix must be confirmed by BALSONS management. The system must support maker-checker controls and prevent a user from approving their own transaction where policy requires separation.
8. GOOGLE SHEETS DATABASE — PROPOSED STRUCTURE
This is a proposed logical schema. Final columns, data types, formulas and relationships must be mapped against the authoritative source workbook and payroll rules before database initialization. No existing live data should be overwritten during migration.
9. DATA MIGRATION AND HISTORICAL PRESERVATION
Identify the authoritative current employee/payroll workbook and approved historical periods before migration.
Preserve employee IDs, employee names, historical salary structures, attendance, payroll outputs, payslip references and existing records as required by the approved migration plan.
Do not silently normalize, rename or discard historical values; map exceptions to a migration issue register.
Run source-to-target record counts, employee ID reconciliation, month-wise totals and payroll control totals.
Keep imported historical periods read-only unless an authorized correction process is approved.
Maintain migration logs, rejected rows, duplicate IDs and reconciliation evidence.
No production migration until a backup and rollback plan has been tested.

10.2 Git and release requirements
Private GitHub repository preferred; never commit employee data, payroll exports, payslips or personal documents.
Protected main branch; feature branches and pull requests.
Tag tested releases and maintain change log/release notes.
CI validates frontend/backend syntax, API contract, tests and payroll regression fixtures.
GitHub Pages deploys approved frontend build.
Apps Script source is deployed separately using clasp and controlled CI or release procedure.
Keep `.clasp.json`, Script IDs where sensitive, OAuth tokens, credentials and environment files out of public Git.
Store deployment credentials in GitHub Actions Secrets or approved secret storage.
Separate staging and production Apps Script projects/deployments and Sheets databases where practical.
Support rollback of frontend and Apps Script backend to compatible known-good releases.
10.3 GitHub Pages
Responsive HTTPS frontend for desktop, tablet and mobile.
No secret or payroll data embedded in static files.
Environment-specific backend endpoint configuration without exposing credentials.
Show application version/build identifier.
GitHub Pages is frontend hosting only; it is not the payroll database or authentication authority.
11. APPS SCRIPT BACKEND REQUIREMENTS
Modular backend services and versioned API contract.
Server-side role checks, project/employee scope validation and input validation.
Authentication, session expiry, logout, failed-login throttling and audit.
Server-side payroll calculation; browser must never be authoritative for pay values.
LockService or equivalent concurrency protection for payroll runs, IDs and approval transitions.
Batch Sheets reads/writes and optimize for Apps Script quotas and execution limits.
Structured API responses with safe errors and correlation IDs.
Idempotency/double-submit protection for payroll, attendance and advance transactions.
Period locking, approval state validation and immutable audit events.
No stack traces, credentials, salary data beyond permission or internal sheet structures returned unnecessarily.
12. API CONTRACT AND SECURITY
Define a versioned API contract before implementing all screens. Each endpoint must specify method, authentication, role, request/response schema, validation, data scope and audit effect.
Address cross-origin requests, CSRF, session/token storage, replay, brute force, IDOR, XSS, injection and unauthorized file access.
Never expose session tokens in URLs.
Never store passwords in plaintext; use an approved identity mechanism or salted password hash design.
Do not rely on frontend-only access restrictions.
Protect employee bank, identity, salary and HR records with least privilege.
13. PAYROLL INTEGRITY AND RECONCILIATION
Payroll engine must use approved formulas and versioned configuration.
Maintain employee-wise calculation trace: input values, earning components, deductions, recovery and net result.
Provide pre-run validation and exception reports before calculation.
Reconcile employee count, gross, deductions, advance recovery and net totals.
Compare selected payroll results against authoritative reference calculations during UAT.
Regression test historical sample periods whenever payroll formula code changes.
Any formula change requires impact analysis, approval and new test evidence.
Do not claim payroll accuracy until representative source periods have been reconciled.
Known historical payroll workbook structures previously referenced include EMPLOYEE DATABASE, MONTHLY SALARY SHEET, monthly sheets such as MAY-26 and JUNE 26, and PAYLIP TEMPLATE. These are source-workbook references for mapping, not a mandate to reproduce Excel tabs as Google Sheets tabs without a data-mapping review.

20. FINAL REQUIREMENT STATEMENT
BALSONS HR & PAYROLL MANAGER shall be a GitHub-hosted HTML web application with Google Apps Script backend and Google Sheets live database. It shall provide five-role access control; employee onboarding/master data; project/site allocation; attendance and monthly summaries; overtime; leave; advances and deductions; salary structures; server-side payroll processing; maker-checker approvals and period locking; individual and batch payslips; HR documents; exit/final settlement; dashboards; reports; audit; and backup/recovery.
The system is online-first and shall use GitHub for source control and frontend hosting, Apps Script for all protected backend operations and Google Sheets as the live database. Excel is not the runtime database in this architecture. Historical records must be preserved and reconciled during approved migration. Payroll formulas must be verified against the authoritative BALSONS source workbook and approved policy before production use. No payroll accuracy or production readiness may be claimed until UAT and management sign-off.
